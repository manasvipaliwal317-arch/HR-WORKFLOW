Implementation Roadmap

Our 6-Step Security Lifecycle

From initial asset discovery to continuous posture evolution.

01

Step 01 — Understand

Understand organizational objectives, compliance scope, operational constraints, and critical data flows to establish the assessment baseline.

02

Step 02 — Assess

Identify potential vulnerabilities, misconfigurations, and architectural weaknesses across networks, applications, and endpoints.

03

Step 03 — Protect

Recommend and assist in deploying appropriate security controls, cryptographic safeguards, firewall policies, and access restrictions.

04

Step 04 — Monitor

Encourage continuous telemetry analysis, anomaly detection, and ongoing situational awareness to spot suspicious activities early.

05

Step 05 — Respond

Develop structured incident response playbooks for rapid containment, evidence preservation, and business recovery during unexpected anomalies.

06

Step 06 — Improve

Continuously strengthen organizational security postures through updated risk reviews, post-incident learnings, and technology modernizations.
