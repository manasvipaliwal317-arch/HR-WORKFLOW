Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026 • Tech Innovations Inc., Khandwa, MP, India

1. Introduction

Tech Innovations Inc. ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting organizational and personal data. This Privacy Policy explains our data collection, usage, and safeguarding practices when you visit our website or engage our cybersecurity consulting services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide through our consultation request forms, email communications, and job application submissions (including name, email address, phone number, resume text, and technical environment inquiries).

3. How We Use Collected Data

  • To respond to security assessment requests and technical inquiries.
  • To evaluate applicant resumes and schedule recruitment interviews.
  • To maintain the operational integrity and security of our web infrastructure.

4. Confidentiality & Security Standards

As a cybersecurity enterprise, all diagnostic information, consultation requests, and candidate data are protected using cryptographic encryption standards and restricted role-based access controls. We do not sell or monetize personal or business contact data to third parties.
